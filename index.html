Privacy Policy and Terms of Use · Last updated 30 August 2026

JustOne does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. Everything you record - the niyam you take and whether you kept it - is stored on your phone and never leaves it. We cannot see it, because it is never sent anywhere.

Privacy Policy

What the app stores, and where

All of it stays in the app's private storage on your device.

What Where
The niyam you are currently keeping Encrypted device storage (Keychain / Keystore)
Your history — one entry per day, and how it went A private database on the device
Your reminder time and settings App preferences on the device

What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no email address, no phone number
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting or usage measurement of any kind
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone

Permissions

Permission Why
Notifications Your daily reminder. It is scheduled by your phone and shown by your phone — nothing is sent to us, and there is no push service involved. Decline it and the rest of the app works unchanged.
Vibration A short haptic tap when you confirm a niyam.
Internet Used only to open the voluntary tip page in your browser, if you choose to open it. The app itself makes no network requests.

The Hindi calendar (Panchanga)

The tithi and paksha shown in the app are calculated on your device from a fixed reference location (Ujjain). The app does not request or use your location.

Supporting JustOne

The Support screen has an optional tip button. Tapping it leaves JustOne and opens a payment page hosted by Razorpay, currently settling to the merchant Fixifaimart. Anything you enter there is handled by Razorpay under their own privacy policy, not ours. We never see your card, UPI, or bank details, and we receive no personal information from a tip. Nothing in the app is paid, and nothing is withheld behind a payment.

Children

JustOne is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and because it collects no data at all, it holds no information about anyone of any age.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, erasure and portability all concern data a company holds about you. We hold none. Your history is yours, on your phone, and only you can read it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app. That removes every niyam, every day of history, and every setting, permanently and immediately. There is nothing on any server to delete and nothing to request from us. You can also clear the app's storage from your phone's Settings → Apps → JustOne → Storage to start again without uninstalling.

Because your history lives only on your phone, we cannot recover it. If you uninstall, change phones, or clear the app's storage, it is gone.

If this ever changes

Should a future version add optional sign-in or backup, it will be exactly that — optional — and this page and the app's Play Store data-safety disclosure will be updated before that version is released. Continuing to use the version you have will never start collecting anything.
